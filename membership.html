Freeze / pause

  • $31/mo, covers the whole calendar month
  • No proof needed — up to 12 times
  • Medical freeze (doctor's memo) → $31 waived
  • Inform staff before the 25th of the prior month
  • Freezing extends your contract — e.g. freeze 2 months on a 24-month plan → contract becomes 26 months

Class booking rules

  • Cancellation window: up to 2 hours before class time
  • Waiting list shown in-app
  • If pulled off waiting list, cancel within 30 min of class start time
  • Penalty: booking suspension after 4 no-shows/late-cancels within a 30-day cycle
  • Instructor's contact can check you in, or call front counter to check in
Cheat code: if running late, just arrive within class timing and check in at the front counter — this clocks you in too.
